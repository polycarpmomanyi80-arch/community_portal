Your privacy matters

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Smart Community Reporting helps residents share local concerns and follow community responses. This policy explains the information the app handles and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Account details: first, second, and last name; email address; phone number; ID number; and password credentials managed by Supabase Authentication.
  • Community content: report title, category, address entered by you, GPS coordinates captured when you submit each report, description, shared thoughts, and report responses.
  • Optional report attachments: photos, video, audio, and documents that you choose to upload or record.
  • Basic technical information needed to authenticate your account and operate the service.

When you submit a report, the app requests device location permission and records your GPS coordinates with the report. You must allow location access to submit a report. You can use your device or browser settings to revoke location permission; if permission is denied or location is unavailable, the report cannot be submitted until GPS access is available.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to create and secure accounts, display community reports and responses to signed-in users, manage reports, and operate the service. Supabase provides authentication, database, serverless function, and file-storage services on our behalf.

Community reports, including their address and GPS coordinates, and shared thoughts are available to signed-in users of the service. Registered phone numbers are shown only to administrators and are used to identify the report's registered sender, not to determine location. Report maps are embedded from OpenStreetMap when opened; loading a map may share your device's network information with that provider. Do not include private or sensitive information in a report or attachment unless it is necessary for resolving the issue.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Account and community data are processed by the service infrastructure used to operate the app. We use authenticated access controls for account and report operations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so protect your password and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Retention and account deletion

Your account information is retained while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated profile, submitted reports, attachments, and shared thoughts from the signed-in app using Delete account, or use the public account deletion page. Account deletion cannot be undone. Technical backups may take additional time to expire under the service provider’s backup cycle.

Your choices

  • You can choose whether to add optional media or documents to a report.
  • You can revoke location permission in your device or browser settings, but GPS access is required to submit reports.
  • You can revoke camera or microphone permission in your device settings. These permissions are only used when you choose to capture report evidence.
  • You can request account and associated-data deletion in the app or through the public deletion page.

Children’s privacy

The service is intended only for adults aged 18 or older. People registering must confirm that they are at least 18. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us so we can review and remove it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
