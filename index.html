Fair reporting and accountability

  • A report is an allegation, not proof that someone is guilty.
  • Do not threaten, harass, expose private information about, or punish anyone through this app.
  • The app and its users do not decide guilt or impose punishments. Only a competent authority may make a finding and impose a penalty under applicable law after a fair process.
  • Any penalty must be determined by that authority under the law and the facts of the case. These rules apply regardless of the type of punishment being considered.
